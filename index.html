This page knows more
about you than your name.

Before you click anything, your browser answers about two dozen questions — what your graphics card is, how your text renders, what your audio stack adds to a tone. Every ad network asks them too. This page asks yours out loud, then shows what the answers are worth in bits.

It is not a name

None of this identifies you personally, and it changes when you switch browsers, install a font or update a GPU driver. It is a temporary serial number, not a permanent ID.

It is enough to link sessions

Clearing cookies does not clear this. Two visits a week apart still look like the same machine, which is exactly what a fingerprint is for.

Bits, not certainty

The count is an upper bound, because signals correlate: a Mac has a Mac GPU and Mac fonts. Published studies put realistic uniqueness between 1 in 3,000 and 1 in 300,000.
